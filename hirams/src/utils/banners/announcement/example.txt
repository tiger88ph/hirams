<h2>New Feature: Management Dashboard</h2>
<p>A brand new Dashboard has been added to give Management a real-time overview of the system at a glance.</p>

<h3>Overview Metrics</h3>
<p>At the top of the Dashboard, you'll now see live counts for key entities across the system:</p>
<div class="status-flow">
  <span class="chip chip-blue">Users</span>
  <span class="arrow">→</span>
  <span class="chip chip-violet">Companies</span>
  <span class="arrow">→</span>
  <span class="chip chip-amber">Clients</span>
  <span class="arrow">→</span>
  <span class="chip chip-teal">Suppliers</span>
</div>

<h3>Ongoing Transactions Panel</h3>
<div class="callout">
  <p>See all <strong>active (non-archived)</strong> transactions broken down by status stage, plus a <strong>"Needs Attention"</strong> worklist highlighting the transactions with the closest due dates — so nothing falls through the cracks.</p>
</div>

<h3>Transactions by Month</h3>
<p>A new bar chart shows transaction volume across the year, with a year selector and an automatic <strong>peak month</strong> callout so you can spot busy periods instantly.</p>

<h3>Revenue, Expenses &amp; Profit</h3>
<div class="callout">
  <p>Track <strong>Revenue</strong>, <strong>Expenses</strong>, and <strong>Profit</strong> side by side in a monthly chart, complete with running totals for the selected year.</p>
</div>

<h3>Employee Performance</h3>
<p>A new performance section tracks how each active employee is handling transactions:</p>
<div class="status-flow">
  <span class="chip chip-green">Transactions Handled</span>
  <span class="arrow">→</span>
  <span class="chip chip-blue">Avg. Business Hours per Step</span>
  <span class="arrow">→</span>
  <span class="chip chip-amber">Speed Ranking</span>
</div>
<div class="callout">
  <p>Employees are ranked by <strong>volume</strong> (transactions handled) and by <strong>speed</strong> (average processing time), giving a clear picture of both output and efficiency. Only active, non-Management staff are included in these calculations.</p>
</div>

<p class="footer-note">If you have any questions about the new Dashboard, please reach out to your team lead.</p>
